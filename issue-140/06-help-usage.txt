  hq kill NAME [-y]                end that agent's Claude session; the sandbox stays
  hq stop [-y]                     end all agents; sandboxes stay
  hq sandbox rm|restart REPO|SANDBOX [-y] remove or restart a repository's sandbox
  hq restore [REPO]                relaunch the agents tmux lost (a host restart), continuing their conversations
hq new also takes --setup NAME: the agent runs in the repository's sandbox of that setup, from "sandboxes" in preferences.json (default: "sandbox").
hq sandbox also takes --setup NAME (the repository's sandbox of that setup, instead of its default) or --all-setups (every sandbox of the repository).
hq: no repository 'nope' (see hq ls)
exit 2
